Monage

Monage is a personal finance management app for the web and Android. It helps you record income, expenses, transfers, budgets, and savings across multiple books, with clear visual reports of your money flow.

Monage offers an optional backup feature. When you choose to connect Google Drive, the app requests the drive.appdata scope so it can store, list, download, and delete your own backup files in a hidden application folder on your Google Drive. Monage never accesses any other files in your Drive, and this data is used only to provide the backup and restore feature — it is never sold or used for advertising.
